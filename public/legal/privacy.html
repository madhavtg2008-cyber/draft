Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Tabellio is a drafting workspace that law firms use to prepare documents with their clients. This policy explains what information Tabellio holds and how it is used.

What we collect

Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only your name and email address from them. We never receive your Google or Apple password.

Your work: the matters, drafts, files, remarks and edit history you or your law firm add to Tabellio.

Technical data: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, show you the matters shared with you, record who made which change, and send emails about your matters (such as invitations and updates to the clean draft).

Who can see your information

Each matter is private. It is visible only to the law firm's team members with access to it and to the people the firm has shared it with. The team draft is never shown to clients. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

We use service providers to host the website, store data and send email. They process information only on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep matters and documents for as long as the law firm keeps them in Tabellio. When a matter is deleted, its drafts and history are removed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by contacting the law firm that invited you.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your information, contact the law firm that invited you to Tabellio.
